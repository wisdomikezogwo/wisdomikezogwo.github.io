\section{\sc Research Interests}
I build toward \textit{autonomous medical intelligence} --- systems that perceive the body across imaging scales, ground what they see, reason like experts, and improve through interaction, toward the physical dexterity needed for surgical manipulation of soft tissue. Concretely: Multimodal Representation \& Generative Modeling, Multi-Agent Reasoning, Reinforcement Learning \& Reward Modeling, Data Curation, Physical/Embodied AI, and AI for Science (Medical).
